## Overview

A companion device/app built for Az's daughter. The hologram-style display is purely cosmetic — a gimmick layer, not the core of the project. The real work is a swappable "brain" layer: different personas and skillsets can be loaded into the companion depending on what's being practiced or taught.

It should be overt and transparent that this is a companion reporting back to Az — not something hidden from his daughter.

## First Brain: Negotiation Technique Practice

The first scoped brain/persona is built around negotiation techniques — giving his daughter a way to practice negotiation skills through conversation with the companion.

## Status

As of October 11, 2026: concept is scoped but not built. None of the following exist yet:

- Voice cloning
- Visual avatar / hologram display
- Conversation logic for the negotiation brain
